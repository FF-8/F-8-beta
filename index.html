F-8

An assistant that lives on your Mac. It listens for its name, uses your apps for you, and asks before anything that matters.

Download F-8 for Mac F-8-0.1.dmg · 11.6 MB · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Install

  1. Open F-8-0.1.dmg from your Downloads folder and drag F-8 onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open F-8 from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app for malware, because this test build isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the F-8 message. Confirm with your password. You only do this once.

If there's no Open Anyway button, paste this into Terminal, then open F-8 again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/F-8.app

First run

  • F-8 starts with a short setup: a name for your agent, a little about you, a daily spending cap and a brain.
  • Brain: on a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on, F-8 uses Apple's on-device model for free. Otherwise choose Add your own and paste an API key (Gemini has a free tier) or connect Ollama.
  • macOS asks for each permission (microphone, speech recognition, automation, and so on) the first time F-8 needs it.
  • Everything F-8 remembers stays on your Mac. You can see or delete it in Settings.

What to try

  • Type a request, or turn the mic on and say the agent's name, then what you want.
  • “Remind me to call Grandma at 6”, “What's on my calendar tomorrow?”, “Open Safari and search for the weather in Vienna”.
  • Click the orb for Afterburner (tasks, spend, memory). Double-click it for Standby.
  • Press ⌥⌘F8 from anywhere to bring F-8 up.

Feedback

This is an early test build, so things will break. When something does, note what you asked, what F-8 did, and take a screenshot if you can, then send it to whoever shared this page with you.
